Why We Built It

One system, from work order to airworthiness release

As a CAAT-certified AMO, keeping accurate, traceable maintenance records isn't optional — it's the job. We couldn't find a system built for organizations our size, so our engineering team built one. Fleet Manager now runs our own fleet, and we're opening it to other MRO and continuing airworthiness organizations.

What's Inside

Everything a maintenance organization needs to track

Client & Contracts

Track aircraft owners and the contracts that define your scope of work.

Aircraft Records

Registration, airframe hours, cycles, and status in one place.

Component Traceability

Full life-cycle log — installed, removed, overhauled, scrapped.

Work Orders

Scheduled and unscheduled maintenance from open to sign-off.

Defect Tracking

Log, classify by severity, and track every defect to closure.

Inventory

Parts stock levels with low-stock alerts and supplier records.

Personnel & Certification

License numbers, type ratings, and expiry tracking.

Maintenance Programme

Task intervals by hours, cycles, or calendar — due-status at a glance.

Document Control

Manuals, logbooks, and certificates with expiry tracking.
