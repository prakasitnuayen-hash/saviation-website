What We Do

Full-scope maintenance for general aviation aircraft

Certified for aircraft up to 5,700 kg, including Cessna 150 · 152 · 172 · 182
01

Scheduled Aircraft Inspections

We perform routine checks based on aircraft maintenance programs at intervals of 50, 100, and 200 hours, following manufacturer-approved standards for aircraft such as Cessna 150, 152, 172, and 182.

02

Line Maintenance Support

Our experienced team provides professional ground support for aircraft pre-flight and post-flight activities, to guarantee operational readiness and safety before every departure.

03

Continuous Airworthiness Management

We offer ongoing technical support and documentation services to ensure your aircraft remains in compliance with airworthiness standards at all times — the same system now available as our Fleet Manager platform.

04

Specialized Services

From minor repairs to structural inspection, composite works, and paint touch-ups, our certified engineers meet all maintenance demands with precision and speed. Our facility at Best Ocean Airpark is fully equipped with modern tools, dedicated hangar zones, and skilled personnel.
