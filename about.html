About Saviation

Bringing international maintenance standards to Thai general aviation

Photo — hangar exterior, Saviation signage

SAVIATION Co., Ltd. is a Civil Aviation Authority of Thailand (CAAT) certified Aircraft Maintenance Organization (AMO 0755), located at Best Ocean Airpark, Samut Sakhon. We provide professional and reliable maintenance services in full compliance with TCAR Part 145 regulations, for small aircraft up to 5,700 kg — particularly Cessna 150, 152, 172, and 182.

Our Vision

“To be a leading international aircraft maintenance organization for small aircraft, with the highest safety standards and excellence in quality, driving the sustainable growth of general aviation.”

Message From The Executive

On behalf of SAVIATION Co., Ltd., I am immensely proud that we have received certification from the Civil Aviation Authority of Thailand (CAAT) as an Aircraft Maintenance Organization (AMO 0755). This accreditation is a testament to our standards and commitment to providing maintenance services for small aircraft with a maximum weight of 5,700 kilograms, particularly Cessna models 150, 152, 172, and 182.

We fully recognize that safety is the paramount concern in aviation. Therefore, SAVIATION Co., Ltd. strictly adheres to the maintenance standards stipulated in TCAR Part 145 and places great emphasis on every step of our operations. This ensures that every aircraft leaving our maintenance facility is in the safest possible condition for flight operations.

Our vision is to bring international standards and safety to small aircraft maintenance in Thailand, thereby building trust with our customers and contributing to the stable and sustainable growth of the Thai aviation industry.

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to all our customers for their trust in our services. I pledge that SAVIATION Co., Ltd. will continue to strive for excellence and continuously improve our services to best meet your needs.

— Executive Management, SAVIATION Co., Ltd.
